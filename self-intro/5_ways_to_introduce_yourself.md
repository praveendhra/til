# 1. About you

  This is where you're gonna tell them what you can do for them...
  
  I help [target audience] achieve [a benefit they desire]
  ex: I help worried job hunters confidently explain why they're the ideal candidate to hire.

  Here, the benefit answers your listener's question.. so you need to identify the question...

# 2. Promise a breakthrough

  I help [target audience] achieve [a benefit they desire] without [negative consequence]

  ex: I help established brands in competitive markets rapidly reach new audiences without increasing marketing spend.

# 3. Not career-focused?
  Say you're in a career pivot.. or a student or something else... you can use either of the below
  - Passion
  - Mission
  - Strength

## 3.1 Passion

  I'm passionate about [something I value] to achieve [something my target audience values]
  
  ex: I'm passionate about helping people in need and creating opportunities that change lives.

## 3.2 Strength

   I'm known for [my strength] to achieve [something my target audience values]

   ex: I'm known for my critical thinking and turning information into actionable insights.

## 3.3 Mission

   I'm on a mission to [ABC] to achieve [something my target audience values]

   ex: I'm on a mission to bridge the healthcare divide and make a lasting difference for vulnerable communities.

  
The trick here is that different audiences value different things, so an intro that interests one person may not interest another
