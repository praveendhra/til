This is a guide to introduce yourself in under 20 words...

## Secret 1:  Think sound bite
       - people have super low attention span.. so your first method is to meticulously plan earlier and tell about yourself in under 20 words.. super quick.
## Secret 2:  Don't talk about yourself
       - About me (I am a award-winning best-selling author) Vs About you(I help new authors get published faster)
       - basically.. how do they benefit from.. thats what makes them curious.. so about you changes from person to person
